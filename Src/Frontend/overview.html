Project at a glance

What EpiShield AI is designed to support.

Forecast disease activity

Use historical case data to help estimate how epidemic activity may change over time.

Identify high-risk regions

Bring regional case and population information together to highlight areas that need attention.

Plan limited resources

Compare allocation scenarios and use priority scores to support preparedness planning.
